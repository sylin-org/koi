   including separate condition, identity, reachability, encryption and authority
   facts with serde round-trip coverage.

   `koi-config::installation` at `state/installation.json`, rejecting unsupported
   schemas rather than treating them as empty.

   projector. It will subscribe before its initial read, use versioned SHA-256

   explicit withdrawal, keep IPv6 interface scope, and derive actions only from
   current evidence and endpoint semantics.

   and expose the catalog through `KoiStatus.catalog`. Dashboard, embedded and
   automation inventory will serialize that exact snapshot without rebuilding it.

   reorder/reset, freshness, watcher closure/restart, bounds and API-only action
   fixtures plus composition and architecture checks.
6. Reconcile the final contract/type inventory, run focused tests, format,
   architecture/document guards, locked workspace checks/tests and strict Clippy.
