# Independent operator trials

Learn whether Koi removes enough recurring container/LAN work to earn continued
use. Begin formative observation before the trusted extension or whole-desktop
migration is complete. Reuse these sessions to evaluate the
[core experience](../ux/README.md); record which actual surface was tested.

## Participants and task

Recruit ten independent operators with an existing recurring problem and a
Windows/Linux container environment within the declared scope. Implementers,
automated fleet agents and repeated sessions with the same person are not
independent participants. Explain the artifact's preview status and limits.

Use the task: label a container, open its actual application from another
computer, and verify correct behavior after recreation/removal. Study trusted
HTTPS separately when the selected artifact completes that workflow.

## Observation record

Use a pseudonymous participant ID and voluntary consent. Record:
- the recurring problem and current alternative;
- coarse OS/runtime/network shape without personal addresses or secrets;
- exact artifact and instructions supplied;
- start/end time, outcome and first failure stage;
- each instruction clarification, manual intervention and maintainer rescue;
- what the operator believes Koi does and what remains their responsibility;
- support minutes and willingness to keep using it for this actual task.

For an unassisted attempt, let the participant use the published documentation
without coaching. Intervene if needed, but record it; a rescued success is a
different outcome. A participant may stop or decline follow-up.

## Experience observation

For the selected preview UI, observe whether the operator can find the service,
choose the intended address, open the application, inspect the relevant evidence
and recover from a representative failure. Record wrong turns, hesitation,
unrecognized controls and each intervention, alongside task completion/time.
Ask the operator to explain what “Found” or “Responding” establishes and where
the check ran. Separately ask what private-space membership says about application
access and browser HTTPS; do not coach the distinction before recording their answer.

After the attempt, ask what felt reassuring, frustrating or unexpectedly useful.
Use those explanations to improve the experience; aesthetic approval alone is
not successful activation. Optional simulated concept sessions inform design but
do not count as real artifact attempts, independent application proof or U06.

L06/U06 require at least five recorded L05 attempts on the selected delivered UI.
Earlier CLI observations remain in the cohort records, but cannot substitute for
that UI sample. Multiple sessions with one person do not create new participants.
Recheck changed paths before accepting the affected UX receipt.

## Thirty-day follow-up

Ask whether Koi still serves a recurring task, which task, what broke, support
needed, and what they used instead if they stopped. Record non-response as
unknown. Measure from that participant's first observed attempt, not the release
date. The primary denominator includes all ten qualified operators, including
people who never activated. Report activated-user retention separately; never
remove activation failures to inflate the retained-use rate.

Initial exploratory thresholds:
- at least 7 of 10 complete the chosen task without live rescue;
- at least 6 of 10 report recurring use at 30 days;
- at least two explain a concrete advantage over their previous setup.

Report all ten outcomes and missing data. These thresholds guide investment;
they are not market-size estimates or permission to generalize to every platform.

## Decisions

If activation fails, fix the observed task/installation obstacle before widening
reach. If activation succeeds but retention fails, examine frequency, value and
the incumbent approach before adding features. If users retain discovery but
avoid trust, simplify the default experience. If integration maintainers show
stronger repeat use, consider concentrating on the API/embedded path.

Use aggregate, consented evidence in public examples. Do not add mandatory
telemetry or contact people from a drafted plan without explicit authorization.
