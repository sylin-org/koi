# Curator briefing requirements

Koi is an open-source container/LAN project for technically capable operators.
Its release story is a real second-device application and correct service
lifecycle, with optional trusted HTTPS as a separate verified milestone.

Certmesh is a visible second product pillar: private-space identity and trust,
including locally governed enrollment, renewal and signed membership information
for participating applications. Exact service-name HTTPS has its own release gate.

Prepare a curator briefing from the actual publication receipt:
- one sentence stating the task and audience;
- exact release/download and versioned quickstart;
- a short reproducible demo with tested host/runtime/client versions;
- known DNS, trust and network prerequisites;
- independent usage evidence, with missing data and support limits;
- maintainer contact and clear open-source licensing.

Do not claim uniqueness against OrbStack, mkdev, Caddy or Tailscale without
specific comparative evidence. Do not contact a curator from this checklist
without explicit communication authority.
