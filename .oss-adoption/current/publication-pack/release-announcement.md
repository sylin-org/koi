# Release announcement requirements

Author the announcement from an actual accepted publication receipt. No focused
preview version or release date is invented in this document.

Include:
- the immutable version and direct release link;
- the container-to-second-device task completed by that artifact;
- supported host/runtime/client rows and a copyable versioned quickstart;
- restart/removal/recovery behavior demonstrated by the fixture;
- known DNS, multicast, firewall and optional trust prerequisites;
- upgrade, verification, rollback and issue-reporting links;
- the specific kind of independent feedback requested.

Present Certmesh as private-space identity and trust using the selected artifact's
documented capabilities. Distinguish member identity and renewal from the separate
exact service-name HTTPS milestone; a certificate alone does not configure an
application's access rules or the client's trust.

Use [the claim contract](../claim-ledger.md) and
[release gates](../../../docs/launch/README.md). A prepared text is not a public
announcement; sending to a community requires explicit communication authority.
