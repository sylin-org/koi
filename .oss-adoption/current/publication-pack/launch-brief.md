# Release briefing

The launch owner assembles this brief at the selected release gate.

Use [readiness](../../../docs/launch/README.md) for facts and
[Epic 004](../../../fleet/epics/004-container-lan-adoption.md) for acceptance.

The brief must identify the exact artifact, who it helps, the complete task,
tested environments, prerequisites, limits, upgrade/rollback and support path.
Include the publication receipt and independent post-publication install.

Lead with the container application opened from a second device and its lifecycle.
Name Certmesh as the private-space identity and trust pillar, using the selected
artifact's documented membership, renewal and application-integration capabilities.
Keep exact service-name HTTPS claims tied to the trusted-service acceptance cases.
Prepared channels and development interfaces are not release features.
