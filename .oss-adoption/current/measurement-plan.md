# Measure adoption

Follow the canonical [cohort protocol](../../docs/launch/cohort.md).
Record task completion, interventions, failure stage, support minutes and
30-day recurring use for ten independent qualified operators.

Use first observed attempt as the clock for every participant. Keep all ten in
the primary denominator, including activation failures; report activated-user
retention separately and missing responses as unknown.

The exploratory targets are 7/10 unassisted task completion, 6/10 retained use
and two concrete advantages over previous setups. These guide investment rather
than prove market size.

[The public-data snapshot](../../docs/assessment/findings/2026-09-12-public-adoption-snapshot.json)
is a discovery baseline. It is not a live monitor or installation count.
The [launch ledger](../../fleet/launch/LEDGER.md) owns actual cohort counters.
